Why TravelMate? 💖

Most travel planners look like dry spreadsheet software built for boardrooms. TravelMate was born from the nostalgic love of vintage paper travel diaries, sticky tape, instant photo stamps, and colorful markers.

We believe a trip begins the minute you dream of it, scribble packing lists, and map out that bakery you've bookmarked for months.

📌

Scrapbook Philosophy 🌿

  • ✨ Gentle pacing: Focus on enjoying 2-3 heartwarming places rather than rushing everywhere.
  • ☕ Cafe pauses: Always schedule time to sit, write postcards, and watch the locals go by.
  • 🎒 Pack light & smile: Leave space in your bag for quirky flea market treasures.
  • 🌸 Zero algorithmic noise: Simple, intuitive, clean client-side tools that keep planning fun.
